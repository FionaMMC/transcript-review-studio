Before you code an interview or quote a participant, you need to check what was actually said. Transcript Review Studio helps you do that on your own computer.

It uses local speech-to-text to generate multiple candidate transcripts. It compares their wording and uses model confidence scores to highlight passages that may need a closer listen, helping you focus your review.

But you can review any part of the recording, including moments the AI hasn't flagged.

Watch the video, replay a sentence, compare the versions, and edit as you listen. Correct the words, label the speaker, or leave an uncertain passage for later.

When you're ready for qualitative analysis, export a Word transcript, an NVivo-style table, or a spreadsheet.

Keep a before-and-after record of your corrections, so colleagues can see what changed.

Speech recognition runs locally. The app does not upload your recordings or transcripts to cloud services.

Try the included sample, follow the walkthrough, and bring in your next interview.
